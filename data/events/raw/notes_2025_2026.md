closes; use them only as a sanity check. File: `usdkrw_ecb_derived_2024-12_to_2026-09.csv` (through 2026-09-14).

## 4. Structural / institutional changes relevant to the event window

- Onshore trading hours: Seoul USD/KRW market extended to 02:00 KST from 1 July 2024 (external institutions admitted,
  RFIs). Consequence: "night session"/"야간거래" moves (e.g. Jun 4-6, 2026 to 1,540-1,560) occur onshore after the
  15:30 daytime close; news quotes both the 15:30 "주간거래 종가" and the 02:00 final. Event dates in the CSV use KST
  calendar dates of the statement; the Jun 7, 2026 F4 (Sunday) reacts to Fri-night/Sat-early trading.
- Ministry reorganisation: 기획재정부 split effective 1 Jan 2026 - FX policy now under 재정경제부 (MOEF-Economy and Finance);
  Dec 2025 rows say 기획재정부, 2026 rows 재정경제부. Director signing the messages: 김재환 (기재부 국제금융국장, Dec 2025)
  → 이형렬 (재경부 국제금융국장, Jun 2026); BOK 국제국장 윤경수 in both.
- BOK governor: 이창용 (to ~20 Apr 2026) → 신현송 (attended Jun 7, 2026 F4 as governor).
- Government/NPS FX-hedging framework: 4자 협의체 (기재부·한은·복지부·국민연금) "뉴 프레임워크" announced mid-Dec 2025;
  NPS strategic hedging (전략적 환헤지) activated Dec 24, 2025 alongside the verbal intervention. (The BOK-NPS FX swap
  line - US$65bn ceiling, extended through end-2025 - was the vehicle; extension into 2026 not verified.)
- Dec 24, 2025 package also included tax measures on overseas investment ("세제 혜택"/"세제 카드") - details not retrieved.
- WGBI: FTSE Russell inclusion of KTBs was announced Oct 2024 with phased inclusion originally from Nov 2025; in the
  Oct 2025 review the start was moved to April 2026 (verify). Relevant to 2026-Q2 inflow narratives.
- Jun 2026: authorities explicitly targeted offshore NDF speculative trading (Jun 7-8 statements) - a shift from
  supply-demand framing (Nov-Dec 2025: resident outflows, "서학개미") to speculation framing.
- Data caveat: news "종가" = 15:30 KST daytime close unless "야간/새벽 2시" stated.
