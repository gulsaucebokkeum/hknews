# Notes: Korean FX verbal-intervention event database, 2010-01-01 to 2015-12-31

Companion to `events_2010_2015.csv` (28 rows). Compiled 2026-09-27 in a sandbox where
WebFetch/curl to every news and government domain was blocked by the egress proxy and the
session-wide WebSearch budget (200 calls, shared with other agents) was exhausted after 22 of
this task's searches. All rows therefore rest on search-engine result summaries and headlines,
not on full article text. Dates/quotes are only as good as those summaries: confidence
`high` = date and wording corroborated by two or more independent headlines/summaries;
`medium` = one specific source, or minor date ambiguity (print date vs statement date);
`low` = headline-only, date inferred/placeholder, or background knowledge (flagged in `notes`).
