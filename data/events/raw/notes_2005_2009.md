# Notes: Korean FX verbal-intervention events, 2005-01-01 to 2009-12-31

Companion to `events_2005_2009.csv` (26 rows). READ THE CAVEATS IN SECTION 4 BEFORE USING THE CSV.

## 1. Tooling outcome and what the CSV is built from

- WebSearch: the session-wide budget (200 calls, shared with the parent/sibling agents) was exhausted after 22 queries from this agent; the last 8 queued queries (2005-2006 batch, listed below as "not executed") returned "budget exhausted".
- WebFetch / curl: every domain tried was blocked by the egress proxy (hankookilbo, etoday, edaily, hankyung, kmib, koreatimes, koreaherald, yna, namu.wiki, en.wikipedia, kci.go.kr, nkis.re.kr, kcif.or.kr, bis.org, imf.org, korea.kr, eiec.kdi.re.kr, bok.or.kr, english.moef.go.kr, web.archive.org, archive.org, Google cache/News RSS, r.jina.ai). No page text was ever retrieved.
- Therefore each CSV row rests on one of three bases, stated in `confidence`:
  - `high` (7 rows): date and quote/gist come from a search-result AI summary or a dated article headline/URL surfaced this session (URLs in `source_urls`).
  - `medium` (2 rows): well-documented episode (2008-03-17, 2008-07-07) whose intervention is search-corroborated but whose exact wording is from background knowledge.
  - `low` (17 rows): background knowledge only, or a source that gave no day. Statements are prefixed `[unverified paraphrase]` or `[content not recovered]`; month-only items are dated the 1st of the month and marked `PLACEHOLDER` in `notes`, per the task rule. None of these should be used as an event date without verification.
- `usdkrw_level` is the FRED H.10 DEXKOUS noon-NY rate from `data/processed/master_daily.csv` for the event date (or previous trading day, flagged). It is not the Seoul close; where a Seoul close is recalled it is given in `notes` and flagged "memory".

## 3. Chronology of FX stress episodes (USD/KRW, FRED NY-noon; monthly min/max from master_daily.csv)

Officials in post: 재경부 장관 이헌재 (to 2005-03) -> 한덕수 (2005-03 to 2006-07) -> 권오규 (2006-07 to 2008-02); 기획재정부 강만수 (2008-02-29 to 2009-02-09) -> 윤증현 (2009-02-10 on). 1st vice ministers: 박병원 (2005-2007), 김석동 (2007-2008), 최중경 (2008-02 to 07-07), 김동수 (2008-07 to 2009-01), 허경욱 (2009-01 on). 국제업무관리관(차관보) 신제윤 (2008-2011); 국제금융국장 최종구 (2007-2009). BOK governors 박승 (to 2006-03), 이성태 (2006-04 to 2010-03).

- 2005 (appreciation pressure, 1,000 line): started 1,035 (Dec-31-2004 close). 2005-02-22 BOK annual report to parliament mentioned reserve diversification -> global USD sell-off, USD/KRW 1,023 -> 1,005 in one day (this is a reserves statement, excluded by definition; BOK clarified 02-23). Below 1,000 in NY on 03-11 (997.5), 04-25/29, 05-04/11 (997); Seoul closes mostly held 1,000. Rebound to 1,054 (07-08); CNY revaluation 07-21 -> 1,018 (07-22); range 1,010-1,060 for H2; 1,010 at year-end.
- 2006 (break of 1,000 and slide to 920s): 1,000 broken 01-04/05, 973.8 by 01-12, 958.9 on 01-31; 950 broken 04-19 (945), 939.6 on 04-24; year low 927.4 on 05-08; 940-960 through Q3; 913.7 on 12-07 (period low until 2007); 930 at year-end.
- 2007 (900 line): 913.6 on 07-25 and 09-28; subprime jolt 08-16/17 (945-950); 908.9 on 10-26, 903.2 on 10-31 (Seoul intraday below 900 on/around 10-31); 905.2 on 11-07 then 932.8 on 11-21; 935.8 at year-end.

## 4. Known / suspected ACTUAL-intervention episodes (dollar selling = curb depreciation; buying = curb appreciation)

Only 2008-03, 2008-07 and 2008-Q4 are search-corroborated; the rest is background knowledge (verify with BOK reserve data and contemporaneous "당국 개입 추정" reports).

- 2005: intermittent dollar BUYING to defend ~1,000 (Feb-May 2005; heavy NDF/forward activity that later drew 국정감사 criticism over 외평기금 losses). Early-Jan-2005 rise (1,038 -> 1,058 on 01-05/06) coincided with the hawkish FOMC minutes, not necessarily intervention.
- 2006: suspected BUYING around 01-31/02-02 (959 -> 972), 04-24/25, 05-08 to 05-16 (927 -> 945), 12-07/08 (914 -> 920).
- 2007: suspected BUYING 10-31/11-01 at the 900 line (903 -> 907/908).
- 2008-03-17 to 03-25: SELLING after the 12-session surge; NY 1,021 -> 976 (search-corroborated episode, amounts unverified).
- 2008-06 to 2008-07-09: sustained SELLING to curb inflation pass-through; reserves -$10.6bn in July; 07-07 to 07-09 -4.2% (search/date-corroborated).
- 2008-08 to 09: SELLING on the "9월 위기설" spike (09-02/03) and after Lehman (09-16/17: +3.7% then -3.9%).
- 2008-10 to 11: heaviest SELLING of the period ("도시락 폭탄" - lunchtime interventions, search-confirmed term); reserves -$27.4bn in Oct and -$11.7bn in Nov [memory]; plus BOK swap-auction dollar supply and Fed swap drawdowns from 12-04.
- 2009-02-20 to 03-13: SELLING at the 1,500-1,600 peak, notably 03-06 (intraday 1,597 -> close ~1,550).
- 2009-09 to 12: BUYING against won strength (below 1,200 from late Sep; reserves +~$10bn Oct, +~$6.7bn Nov).

Events in the CSV with `actual_intervention_reported=Y` are those where same-day dealer reports of official selling/buying are recalled or corroborated; `U` where unknown.

- 2007: only 권오규's 11-01 tolerance remark is verified; the Oct-31 MOFE response is unverified; nothing for Jul/Sep 2007.
- 2008-H1: dates for 최중경 (03-10) and 강만수 (04-20, 07-23) are inferred from next-day article dates; wording not recovered. 2008-03-17 wording unverified though the episode is corroborated.
- 2008-Q4: the dense cluster of daily MOSF/BOK verbal interventions (10-06 to 11-25, incl. 김동수's "대응능력 의심하지 말라", the 10-08 and 10-28 statements, the 1,500-break statements of 11-20/21/24, and Dec statements) is represented by only a handful of rows; several are undated placeholders. The KCI paper (ART001579846) covering 2008-09 to 2009-04 is the best single source to fill this.
- 2009: 03-02/03-06 wording unverified; 허경욱's Feb-Mar 2009 statements missing; H2-2009 appreciation-curbing statements (윤증현 국감, 신제윤, 허경욱) are month-level placeholders only.
- Time-of-day (`time_kst`) is blank for every row - none of the sources gave times.
- `joint` and `official` flags for 2008-07-07 and 2008-10-08 are tentative.
