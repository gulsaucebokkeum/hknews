## 1. Source conventions and caveats (read before using the CSV)

* Almost all events come from the Hankook Ilbo (한국일보) online archive, whose URLs embed the print-edition
  date (`.../news/article/YYYYMMDD...`). Cross-checks (2001-04-05 briefing reported under 2001-04-06;
  2002-05-27 close reported under 2002-05-28; 2004-11-22 breakfast reported under 2004-11-23; 2004-01-29
  release under 2004-01-30) show **edition date = event date + 1**. Event dates in the CSV were derived that
  way unless the summary stated the day explicitly; each row's `notes` says which.
* Quotes were extracted by the search engine's AI summaries, not from the full article text. Wording is
  therefore plausible but unverified; rows marked `(paraphrase)` or `(headline only)` carry no verbatim quote.
  Verify every quote against the original article (Naver News Library / BigKinds / the paper's archive)
  before using it in the paper.
* `usdkrw_level` values flagged "approx." are from memory or from indirect statements in summaries.
* `confidence`: high = date explicit in source and quote text captured; medium = date inferred from edition
  date and/or quote from summary; low = headline-level or attribution/date uncertain.
* Three rows are deliberately included but flagged for possible exclusion: 2002-01-03 (Jin Nyum on the yen,
  borderline), 2004-01-20 and 2004-03-11 (BOK Governor Park Seung's anti-intervention / "leave it to the
  market" remarks, coded direction=unclear). They are counter-signals rather than interventions.

## 4. Reported ACTUAL-intervention episodes (dollar buying/selling by authorities)

* 2000-11-21 onward: verbal intervention then indirect dollar supply through public institutions and window
  guidance to oil refiners; by 11/24 no action as 1,200 was tested (dollar selling / curb depreciation).
* 2001-04-06 (Fri) ~11:45: ~US$200m supplied as the rate neared 1,360; fell to 1,342.5 — BOK's first direct
  intervention of the episode ("실탄풀었다"); the 4/9 column says direct intervention began "last week".
* 2002-07-08/12: direct and indirect intervention (dollar buying); unplanned 500bn-won 외평채 issued 7/12;
  early issuance of remaining 2.8tn; public enterprises asked to prepay dollar debt and delay foreign borrowing.
* 2003-02-06: 1tn-won 외평채 issue to buy dollars announced; further 4tn considered.
* Jun-Jul 2003: bond-financed dollar buying nearly exhausted the 5tn-won ceiling (raised to 9tn mid-July);
  MSB outstanding passed 100tn won.
* 2003-09-22 to 10-02: ~US$3bn absorbed in ten days defending 1,150.
* Jan-Feb 2004: ~US$1bn bought almost daily; firm line at 1,160 (2/16); 1tn-won 외평채 issue announced.
* 2004-11-10: intervention lifts rate back to 1,110; reserves +US$7.7bn in first half of November.
* 2004-11-22: several rounds of dollar buying as 1,060 threatened; bond ceiling exhausted, BOK money
  creation used.
* 2004-11-26: repeated intervention fails to hold 1,050; November reserves +US$14.2bn, BOK debt +13tn won
  (12/3 edition).

## 6. Coverage gaps (honest assessment)

* Only one newspaper archive (Hankook Ilbo) was searchable; Yonhap, Donga, JoongAng, MK, Hankyung, Hani,
  Reuters and Bloomberg — the usual carriers of same-day 구두개입 wires — were inaccessible. Expect the true
  count of verbal interventions in 2000-2004 to be several times the 19 rows here, especially the many
  routine "예의주시/쏠림 우려" remarks by 변양호 (국제금융국장 2001-04), 김용덕 (국제업무정책관), 권태신
  (국제업무정책관 2003-05, from memory) and 최중경, and BOK 국제국 comments.
* Periods with NO events captured: Jan-Oct 2000; Dec 2000-Mar 2001; May-Dec 2001 (incl. 9/11); Aug-Dec 2002;
  Apr-Aug 2003; Oct-Dec 2003 (apart from 10/2 intervention); Apr-Oct 2004; Dec 2004 statements.
* No BOK-governor statements from the 전철환 era (1998-2002) were found; the 2001-04-05 event is by an
  assistant governor.
* MOEF's own 2004-11/12 press statements (e.g. the formal 구두개입 wording usually issued jointly by MOEF
  and BOK) could not be retrieved; the 11/29 row's attribution is uncertain.
* Times of day are unknown for all events except that 2004-11-22 was a breakfast meeting.
* Exchange-rate levels are closes only where stated; other levels are approximate.
