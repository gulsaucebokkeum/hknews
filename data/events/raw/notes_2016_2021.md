## 3. Actual-intervention episodes (dollar selling/buying reported or disclosed)

- 2016-02-19: joint verbal intervention; actual selling not confirmed in retrieved text (Hankook Ilbo: "당국 개입에도 상승세 못 꺾어").
- 2016-06-24: MOEF explicitly pledged "스무딩 오퍼레이션" (smoothing) — execution not confirmed.

- 2020-12 / 2021-01: edaily "외환당국, 왜 1080원 방어에 전력 투구할까?" (undated) implies sustained dollar buying near 1080.
