# Notes: Korean FX verbal-intervention event database, 2022-01-01 to 2024-12-31

Companion to `events_2022_2024.csv` (34 rows). Built 2026-09-27 in a session where the shared
WebSearch budget (200 calls/session) ran out after 36 of my planned ~62 searches, and every
WebFetch attempt (19 URLs, 17 domains incl. bok.or.kr, mofe.go.kr, eiec.kdi.re.kr, edaily,
etoday, seoul.co.kr, ajunews, hankyung, koreaherald, namu.wiki, wikipedia) was refused by the
egress proxy (EGRESS_BLOCKED). Consequently:

* **2022 is well covered** (25 rows, 14 of them confidence=high with verbatim Korean quotes
  taken from search-result excerpts of edaily / seoul.co.kr / etoday / mt / newspim / KDI).
* **2023 has zero verified rows.** All 2023-targeted searches were rejected by the budget cap.
  One strong indirect finding: the 2024-04-16 official statement was reported as the first
  official 구두개입 since 2022-09-15, so **no bureau-level official verbal intervention occurred in
  2023** (ad-hoc ministerial remarks may still exist — see gaps).
* **2024 is partially covered** (9 rows). 2024-04-16 is verified (high). The Apr-17/18 Korea–Japan
  and US–Japan–Korea statements, Nov-6 and Nov-14 F4/ministerial remarks, and the Dec-4 / Dec-19 /
  Dec-20 F4 statements are from model recall and flagged `low`/`medium`; quotes for those rows
  are explicitly marked `[paraphrase, UNVERIFIED in this session]`. Nothing in the CSV is an
  invented verbatim quote: every row's `statement` is either an excerpt reproduced from a search
  result or is labelled as paraphrase / headline / not captured.

USD/KRW levels: `usdkrw_level` is the Seoul close when an article gave it (stated in `notes`),
otherwise the FRED H.10 NY-noon rate from `data/processed/master_daily.csv` (`usdkrw_fred`).
Levels marked "(recall)" in notes are Seoul closes remembered, cross-checked against FRED/ECB
values in master_daily (all within ~5 won).

Intensity coding follows the brief literally: every joint MOEF–BOK official statement (기재부
국제금융국·한은 국제국 명의 공식 구두개입) is coded 3, F4 statements with concrete measures 3,
"필요시 시장안정조치" 2, "예의주시/경계감/우려" 1.

## 4. Honest assessment of coverage gaps

1. **2023 — no rows.** All 2023 queries were blocked. Likely candidates to verify: Feb-2023 remarks
   as 1,300 was re-crossed (02-17), the March SVB episode (비상거시경제금융회의 2023-03-13/14?),
   the 2023-04 renewal/expansion of the NPS–BOK swap ($35bn) which was an official 외환당국 press
   release, 추경호/이창용 remarks in Aug–Oct 2023 around 1,340-1,363, and any 기재부 1차관
   시장상황점검회의 on 2023-10-04. The strong prior from the 2024-04-16 coverage is that there were
   **no** bureau-level official 구두개입 in 2023.
2. **2024 Jan–Mar, May–Oct — no rows.** Jan-17 (won 1,344) and late-June (1,390) may have had
   1차관-level or F4 remarks; not searched.
3. **2024 Nov–Dec — statements are known to be frequent (F4 met on Nov 6, Nov 14?, Dec 4, 5, 6, 8,
   9, 10, 19, 20, 23?, 27, 30) but only Dec 4/19/20 and Nov 6/14 are itemised, with paraphrased
   wording.** The Dec 27 (Han Duck-soo impeachment; intraday 1,486.7) and Dec 30 statements are
   missing. Per hankyung (2025-12-24) no 국장급 official statement was issued in this period, so
   all Nov–Dec 2024 events are F4/ministerial-level.
4. **2022 gaps:** wording for the 06-16 and 07-28 비상거금 statements (placeholders, low);
   possible July 2022 statement (the search summary claimed a 07-13 구두개입 but the evidence was
   contaminated by 2026 articles — treat as unverified); October 2022 (peak 1,444.2 on 10-25) has
   no official statement found — consistent with reports that authorities relied on actual selling
   plus ministerial remarks; late-Sep (09-28/29, ~1,440) not captured.
5. **Times of day** are captured only for 2022-09-15 (13:20) and for morning-meeting statements
   (08:00 approximate). Official bureau-level statements on 2022-04-25, 06-13, 08-23 and 2024-04-16
   were intraday; exact minutes need the 외환마감/상보 articles.
6. **Speaker names** for the 2022-04-25 statement are unconfirmed (search summary likely conflated
   with 2024-04-16).
7. **actual_intervention_reported** is mostly U; Y is set only where a source reported or the
   official admitted selling (09-15, 09-22, 10-05/06, 10-13 2022; 04-16 2024, 04-25 2022 via
   headline).
8. The rows for the President (2022-08-23 윤석열) and the reserves-focused 2022-10-06 remark are
   borderline under the brief's definition; they are flagged in `notes` and can be dropped.
